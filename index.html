On-Ground Daily SOP & Guides

⚡ 15-Minute Callback Rule

86% of offline sales are lost with zero follow-up. Always call back high-intent missed calls within 15 minutes of store walk-out.

🎯 3-Step Walk-out Recovery Script

  1. Acknowledge & Name: Mention the customer's name and the exact product viewed (+23.6pp conversion lift).
  2. Unblock the Objection: Check warranty, home dimensions, or offer zero-cost EMI.
  3. Clear Next Step: Lock in an appointment or send quotation via WhatsApp immediately.

💬 Common Customer Objections & Responses

"Need to discuss with spouse/manager"

Offer a virtual showroom tour or instant PDF summary to their WhatsApp.

"Price seems high"

Highlight the 10-year warranty, durability score, and ₹0 down payment financing option.
